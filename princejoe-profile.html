Meet The Consultant

Onosedeba Joseph Osas

Property Consultant | REEL Properties — Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos State, Nigeria

"Real estate is not just about property — it is about connecting people with the right opportunities at the right time."

Onosedeba Joseph Osas, popularly known as Prince Joe, is a Nigerian Property Consultant originally from Edo State and currently based in Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos State — the heartland of Nigeria's fastest-growing real estate corridor.

He specialises in property consultancy, real estate investment opportunities, property sales, rentals, and management. With a genuine passion for connecting people with premium property opportunities, Prince Joe is committed to promoting real estate awareness and helping clients make informed, high-value property decisions across Lagos and beyond.

Beyond real estate, Prince Joe has built a growing digital presence through comedy, lifestyle, and motivational content — using platforms like YouTube and TikTok to entertain, inspire, and engage his audience. His personal brand sits at the intersection of property expertise and digital media, amplifying REEL's reach across Nigeria's vibrant content landscape.
