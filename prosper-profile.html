Meet The Innovator

Prosper Mwendwa

Head of Digital & Technology | Royalty Executive Empire Limited (REEL)

"Technology is not just what I do — it is how REEL speaks to the world."

At just 20 years of age, Prosper Mwendwa brings a sharp, forward-thinking edge to the digital operations of Royalty Executive Empire Limited. As Head of Digital & Technology, he is the architect behind REEL's web presence, digital systems, and IT infrastructure — ensuring every platform the company operates on meets the highest standard of precision and performance.

A self-driven innovator with a natural command of modern technology, Prosper designs, builds, and maintains the digital backbone that powers REEL's imperial vision. From website development and UI/UX to IT systems and digital strategy, he operates with the discipline of a veteran and the creativity of a next-generation technologist.

Despite his age, Prosper's approach is anything but junior. He understands that in today's world, a company's digital presence is its first handshake with the world — and he ensures REEL's handshake is nothing short of extraordinary. His work supports every division across the REEL ecosystem, from REEL Tech Hub and REEL Auto Centre to REEL Properties and REEL Branding.

Web Development Building and maintaining REEL's web platforms and digital touchpoints.
IT Infrastructure Managing the technology backbone that keeps REEL's operations running.
Digital Strategy Driving REEL's online identity and digital expansion across Africa and beyond.
