Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does REEL Auto Centre service corporate fleets, or only individual vehicles?

Both. We offer individual executive vehicle care as well as scheduled maintenance contracts for corporate fleets.

Q. What makes this different from a standard car wash or garage?

REEL Auto Centre is positioned as a premium, executive-grade service — detailing, maintenance, and care standards benchmarked against luxury automotive care internationally, not general auto repair.

Q. How does REEL Auto Centre integrate with the REEL family?
